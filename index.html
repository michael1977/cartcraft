Privacy Policy for BuyButtonFlair

Last updated: February 7, 2025

CartCraft operates the BuyButtonFlair Shopify app. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our service.

📋 Information We Collect

When you install and use BuyButtonFlair, we collect the following information:

  • Shop Information: Your shop's name, domain, and Shopify store ID
  • Configuration Data: Button effect settings, colors, animations, and preferences you configure
  • Usage Data: Information about how you use the app (which features you enable, pages visited)
  • Analytics Data: Button impression and click data to help you understand performance

🔒 How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To provide button effect functionality on your storefront
  • Personalization: To save and apply your custom effect configurations
  • Analytics: To show you how your button effects are performing
  • Support: To provide customer support and troubleshoot issues
  • Improvements: To improve our app features and user experience

💾 Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on Gadget.dev infrastructure, which provides:

  • Industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Isolated data storage per merchant
  • Automated backups and disaster recovery
We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

🚫 Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do NOT:

  • Sell your data to third parties
  • Share your data with advertisers
  • Use your data for purposes outside of providing our service

We DO share data with:

  • Gadget.dev: Our hosting and backend infrastructure provider
  • Shopify: As required for app integration and authentication
  • Service providers only when necessary to operate our service (under strict confidentiality agreements)

👤 Your Rights and Data Control

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we store about your shop
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (uninstalling the app automatically triggers data deletion)
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Opt-out: Disable specific features or data collection within the app

🗑️ Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data for as long as you use BuyButtonFlair. When you:

  • Uninstall the app: All your configuration data is deleted within 48 hours
  • Request deletion: We delete your data within 30 days of your request
  • Close your Shopify store: Associated data is automatically deleted

🍪 Cookies and Tracking

BuyButtonFlair does not use cookies in the merchant admin interface. On your storefront:

  • We do not set cookies on your customers' browsers
  • We use Shopify's existing session data to identify your shop
  • Analytics are tracked server-side without customer tracking

👶 Children's Privacy

Our service is intended for merchants, not children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 years of age. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us immediately.

🌍 International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and stored on servers located outside your country of residence. By using BuyButtonFlair, you consent to such transfers. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

⚖️ GDPR Compliance

For merchants in the European Union, we comply with GDPR requirements:

  • Lawful basis: Performance of contract (providing the app service)
  • Data Protection Officer: Available upon request
  • Right to lodge complaints with your local supervisory authority
  • Automated decision-making: We do not use automated decision-making or profiling

📱 Shopify App Store Requirements

As a Shopify app, we comply with:

  • Shopify's API Terms of Service
  • Shopify's App Store Requirements
  • Shopify's Partner Program Agreement

🔄 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Sending an email notification for material changes
  • Displaying a notice in the app for significant updates

Your continued use of BuyButtonFlair after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
